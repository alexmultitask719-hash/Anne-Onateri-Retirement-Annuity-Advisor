What is an Annuity?

When you retire, or reach age 50 (whichever is later), the Pension Reform Act 2004 says you use part of your RSA balance to buy an annuity from a NAICOM-licensed life insurance company.

The insurer then pays you a regular income, monthly or quarterly, for the rest of your life.

Benefits

Income for life

Regular payment till death, however long you live.

Death benefit

A lump sum is paid to your family on the death of the annuitant.

Money-back protection

Refund of purchase money with interest if death occurs before payments begin.

Plan Features

  • Conforms with the provisions of the PRA 2004
  • Nil or guaranteed payment period option
  • Optional retirement age of 45, 50, 55, 60 and 65
  • Increasing annuity option
  • Additional death benefit option
  • Retiree Life Annuity, Deferred and Immediate annuities

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can buy an annuity?

Holders of a Retirement Savings Account (RSA) who have retired or reached age 50, whichever is later.

Can I get my money back after buying?

An annuity is generally not reversible, so it is important to choose the right options before you buy.

What happens if I die early?

You can choose a guaranteed payment period or an additional death benefit so your family is protected.

Will my payments increase?

With the increasing annuity option, payments rise over time to help with inflation.
